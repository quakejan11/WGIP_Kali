## Overview

Wireless Geospatial Intelligence Platform (WGIP) is a centralized wireless data collection, analytics, and reporting platform designed to process wireless survey data collected from authorized field operations.

WGIP imports wireless observations generated by Kismet running on DragonOS and stores every wireless observation inside a PostgreSQL/PostGIS database.

Unlike traditional wireless survey software, WGIP preserves every wireless detection, allowing analysts to review the complete historical timeline of every Wi-Fi network.

---

## Objectives

- Collect wireless observations
- Maintain a historical wireless observation database
- Provide GIS visualization
- Produce analytics dashboards
- Generate intelligence reports
- Support authorized wireless data collection
